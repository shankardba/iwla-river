Nitrate stays low

Good news Nitrate strips have read 2 mg/L at the creek on every visit.

Sampling pointReadingsLowest, mg/LHighest, mg/LYears
IWLAR Seneca Creek17222025–2026
IWLAR Lake17002025–2026

Test strips read in coarse colour steps, so a steady 2 means the creek sits in a low band rather than at exactly 2. That is well below the EPA's 10 mg/L drinking-water limit for nitrate as nitrogen. Strips cannot show small changes, so nitrate is unlikely to explain the loss of sensitive animals on this evidence.
