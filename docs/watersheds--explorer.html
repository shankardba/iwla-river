Watersheds

Watershed explorer

Context Explore 3,433 subwatersheds with surveys, from US regions down to the chapter's own Great Seneca Creek.

Each bubble is a watershed inside the one you are looking at, placed at the average position of its sites and sized by how many sites and surveys it holds. Its colour compares the share of pollution-sensitive animals found with what the expected-score model predicts for each survey's season and setting. Click a bubble or a row to zoom in; at the smallest level each site opens in Google Maps.

Watersheds are USGS hydrologic units (Watershed Boundary Dataset): 2-digit regions, 4-digit subregions, 6-digit basins, 8-digit subbasins, 10-digit watersheds and 12-digit subwatersheds. No map tiles are loaded.

How this was worked out

Sites are placed in USGS hydrologic units by a point-in-polygon test against the Watershed Boundary Dataset. A watershed's figures pool every usable survey from its sites; its gap trend is a seasonal Kendall test on the median gap per season and year, shown only with at least 10 same-season comparisons.
