Road salt reaches the creek every winter

Watch Chloride in the creek climbs from about 85 mg/L in summer to winter peaks of 159, still below the EPA's 230.

159 mg/Lhighest winter chloride in the creek
132 mg/LMaryland Salt Watch median

Since 2024 the chapter has tested its creek and lake for chloride with Salt Watch strips.

Road salt shows up every winter: winter peaks were 2024–25: 145, 2025–26: 159. All readings so far sit below the EPA's chronic aquatic-life level of 230 mg/L and below the Maryland Salt Watch median of 132 mg/L.

Two winters is too short for a trend; more years will show whether the peaks keep rising. Salt is a likely suspect for the loss of sensitive animals, since mayflies and stoneflies are among the first to suffer.
