National picture

Volunteer monitoring took off after 2017

Context The Hub holds 83,297 usable surveys; 7,414 are biological, enough to train a national model.

Salt Watch and Nitrate Watch kits, mailed free to volunteers, account for most of the growth since 2019. The biological surveys, which take training and a net, grow more slowly but carry the ecology.
